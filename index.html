from automation to intelligence

Intelligence changes the unit of automation.

Traditional automation

if x happens → do y
  • Fast and reliable when the situation is known in advance.
  • Best suited to predictable, rules-based work.

Intelligent automation

understand → prioritize → decide → act → escalate
  • Moves into knowledge work, decisions, exceptions, and coordination.
  • Combines AI with workflows, data, actions, and human oversight.
